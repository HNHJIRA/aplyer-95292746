Privacy Policy

How Aplyer.ai Collects, Uses, and Protects Your Information

Aplyer LLC | Effective Date: July 27, 2026

Aplyer LLC ("Aplyer," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website Aplyer.ai and the Aplyer Chrome extension (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, your rights regarding that information, and how we protect it. By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Join our waitlist by submitting your email address
  • Create an account on the Service
  • Upload your resume or writing samples to the Service
  • Contact us by email or through the Service

This may include your name, email address, resume content, professional history, writing samples, and any other content you choose to provide.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the Service, we may automatically collect:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Pages or features of the Service you access
  • Date and time of access
  • IP address
  • Referring URL

We use this information to improve the Service and diagnose technical problems. We do not sell this data.

1.3 Information From Job Applications

When you use the Aplyer Chrome extension on a company careers page, the extension reads the visible text of the form fields on that page, including open-ended question text and the job description, for the purpose of generating answers and scoring your resume match. Job application question text is processed in real time and is not retained by Aplyer servers after the answer is generated. Job description text may be cached in hashed form for up to 30 days to provide consistent scoring results across sessions.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Generate AI-powered answers using your resume and voice profile
  • Send you waitlist updates, early access notifications, and product announcements
  • Respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents

We will never sell your personal information to third parties. We will never use your resume content or writing samples to train AI models beyond the scope of providing the Service to you. We may use anonymized, aggregated patterns from how users edit generated answers to improve our technology. This data is never tied to your identity and cannot be used to identify you.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract: Processing necessary to provide the Service you have requested
  • Legitimate interests: Analytics and security that do not override your rights
  • Consent: Where you have given explicit consent, such as joining the waitlist
  • Legal obligation: Where processing is required by applicable law

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. Waitlist email addresses are retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion. Resume content and writing samples are retained as long as you maintain an active account and are deleted within 30 days of account deletion upon request.

The original resume file you upload is deleted from our systems immediately after the text is extracted from it. We retain only the extracted text, not the document itself.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal data
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to our processing of your data for certain purposes
  • Restriction: Request that we limit how we process your data

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@aplyer.ai. We will respond within 30 days.

6. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect and how it is used, the right to request deletion of personal information, and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To submit a CCPA request, email privacy@aplyer.ai.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information. These include encrypted data transmission using TLS, secure storage of resume content, and limited employee access to personal data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Third-Party Services

The Service integrates with the following third-party services to operate. Each has its own privacy policy. We share only the minimum data necessary to provide the Service: Anthropic (AI model provider; receives resume excerpts and question text to generate answers), Copyleaks and Pangram (AI detection services; receive generated answer text only, not personal information, to produce Human Score ratings), Brevo (email delivery; receives your email address for waitlist and product communications), ZeroBounce (email address validation; receives your email address to verify deliverability), Stripe (payment processing; receives payment information for subscriptions), and Vercel (infrastructure and hosting; hosts the Service and processes requests). We do not share data with third parties beyond what is necessary to provide the Service or as required by law.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by email or by posting a notice on the Service. Your continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
